### 2. Unity Asset Store Comparison

**Unity Asset Store Stats:**
- **11,000+** 3D model packs

- **Earnings Tiers:**
  - Year 1: $1,000-3,000/month (realistic for quality publishers)
  - Top publishers: $30,000+/month

**Lessons for Godot:**
1. Quality + Polish = Success (10-20 hours for simple, 2-6 months for complex)
2. Regular updates critical for visibility
3. Game templates sell well (complete projects > individual assets)
4. Mobile-focused assets in high demand

## 🏆 COMPETITOR ANALYSIS

### Godot Asset Library (Current)

**Strengths:**
- ✅ Free (barrier to entry = 0)
- ✅ Growing community
- ✅ Official support from Godot Foundation
- ✅ MIT licensed (very permissive)

**Weaknesses:**
- ❌ No premium tier (yet)
- ❌ Limited quality control
- ❌ No monetization for creators
- ❌ Smaller user base vs Unity/Unreal

**Opportunity:**
```
Create PREMIUM Godot assets with:
- Professional quality
- Documentation & support
- Regular updates
- Monetization model (one-time or subscription)
```

### Unity Asset Store

**Strengths:**
- ✅ Massive user base (millions of devs)
- ✅ Established monetization
- ✅ High-quality assets
- ✅ Regular sales & promotions

**Weaknesses:**
- ❌ High competition (11,000+ assets)
- ❌ 12% royalty fee
- ❌ Unity pricing changes (trust issues)
- ❌ Discovery difficult for new publishers

### Unreal Marketplace

**Strengths:**
- ✅ High-quality AAA assets
- ✅ 5% royalty (after $1M revenue)
- ✅ Professional market

**Weaknesses:**
- ❌ Smaller indie market
- ❌ Complex royalty model
- ❌ Higher quality bar (harder to enter)
