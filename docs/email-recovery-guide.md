# 📧 Corporate Email Recovery Guide
## Hotmail/Outlook Account Recovery

---

## 🚨 SITUATION
- Manager resigned, cut off all contact
- Recovery email & phone controlled by ex-manager
- Company needs access to corporate email
- Account locked for 3+ months

---

## ✅ RECOVERY OPTIONS (In Order)

### Option 1: Microsoft Account Recovery Form
**URL:** https://account.live.com/acsr

**Information Needed:**

#### Required:
- [ ] Email address to recover
- [ ] Working contact email (for Microsoft to respond)
- [ ] Account creation date (approximate)
- [ ] Last successful login date

#### Strongly Recommended:
- [ ] Previous passwords used
- [ ] Subject lines of recent emails (3-5)
- [ ] Names of folders in inbox
- [ ] Email addresses of contacts
- [ ] Birthdate on account (if set)

#### Additional Proof:
- [ ] Devices used to access account
- [ ] Locations (cities/countries) of login
- [ ] Security questions & answers (if set)

---

### Option 2: Microsoft 365 Business Support
**For:** Custom domain emails (@company.com)

**Contact:**
- URL: https://support.microsoft.com/contactus
- Phone: Varies by country

**Documents Required:**
- [ ] Domain ownership proof (WHOIS, registrar invoice)
- [ ] Business registration documents
- [ ] Previous Microsoft invoices/subscriptions
- [ ] Legal letter from company director

---

### Option 3: Legal Escalation
**If above fails:**

1. **Lawyer Letter**
   - Demand ex-manager return account access
   - Threaten legal action for data theft/sabotage

2. **Police Report**
   - Report as unauthorized access/cybercrime
   - Provide to Microsoft as evidence

3. **Court Order**
   - Subpoena Microsoft for account access
   - Requires lawyer & legal process

## ⚡ IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

### While Waiting for Recovery:

1. **Secure Other Accounts**
   - Change passwords for related accounts
   - Check for password reuse
   - Enable 2FA everywhere

2. **Notify Stakeholders**
   - Inform customers of email change
   - Update email on all platforms
   - Set up temporary communication channel

3. **Create New Email**
   - Setup replacement email immediately
   - Update business cards/website
   - Forward old domain (if possible)

4. **Document Everything**
   - Screenshot recovery attempts
   - Save all correspondence
   - Timeline of events

---

## 🛡️ PREVENTION (For Future)

### Corporate Email Policy:

1. **Shared Recovery**
   - Recovery email: admin@company.com (not personal)
   - Recovery phone: company phone (not personal)

2. **Admin Access**
   - IT admin always has backup access
   - Regular access audits
   - Immediate revocation on resignation

3. **Documentation**
   - All accounts logged in company directory
   - Password manager with shared vault
   - Regular backups of critical emails

4. **Exit Protocol**
   - Account handover checklist
   - Password change on last day
   - Recovery info update before exit
